# PersonSort Setup Guide (For Team Members)

## 1. Download & Install
1. Download the latest **`PersonSort_Setup_x.x.x.exe`** installer from the project's **GitHub Releases** page.
2. Double-click the installer to install **PersonSort** and create a Desktop shortcut (no Python or administrator password required).

---

## 2. First-Launch Security Prompt (One-Time Only)
Because PersonSort is packaged directly for your team without a paid commercial certificate, your computer may show a one-time prompt the very first time you open it:

- **Windows ("Windows protected your PC"):**
  1. Click **More info**.
  2. Click **Run anyway**.
- **macOS ("cannot be opened because it is from an unidentified developer"):**
  1. **Right-click** (or Control-click) the **PersonSort** app icon and choose **Open**.
  2. Click **Open** in the dialog.

After doing this once, PersonSort opens normally with a double-click.

---

## 3. Connect Dropbox (One-Click)
1. When PersonSort opens in your browser, a **"Welcome to PersonSort"** popup will appear.
2. Click **Connect Dropbox**.
3. Sign in to your Dropbox account in the popup window and click **Allow** — the popup will close automatically and remember your login so you don't need to sign in again.
4. **Shared Team Folders:** If someone invited you to a shared Dropbox folder, make sure you have clicked **Accept / Add to Dropbox** on that invite in Dropbox first. If permission is missing, PersonSort will show a clear yellow prompt reminding you to accept the invite.

---

## 4. First-Run AI Model Download
The **very first time** you scan photos, you may see:

> *First-time setup: downloading AI models (this only happens once)...*

Please leave the window open for 1–2 minutes while it finishes downloading the face/jersey recognition models. Every scan after that starts immediately.

---

## 5. Sorting & Saving Photos
- **Input Source:** Drag and drop local photos / `.zip` archives on the left card, **or** click **Browse Dropbox Folders** on the right card (non-photo files inside a folder are highlighted in red) and click **Load & Scan Dropbox Folder**.
- **Review:** Click any photo to toggle bounding boxes on/off, check face match suggestions, rename/merge player folders, or merge `#` jersey folders.
- **Export:** Click **Create Real Folders** to choose either a **Local Computer Folder** or a **Dropbox Folder** (with **Copy** or **Move** handling).

---

## 6. Closing PersonSort
- Closing the small **PersonSort** control window (**X** button or **Quit PersonSort**) cleanly stops PersonSort in the background. You can also click **Open PersonSort in Browser** or **Copy Link** in that window anytime if you accidentally close your browser tab.
- Clicking **Quit App** in the top-right corner of the PersonSort browser page also stops the app and closes the control window.
